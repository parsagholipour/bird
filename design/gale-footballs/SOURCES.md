# Gale footballs: reference

The footballs a gale throws over Brazil (`lib/game/gale_football_art.dart`)
are our own Canvas drawing. One reference was used, for inspiration only;
none of its geometry is copied into the game.

- `reference-openclipart-305104-classic-football.svg`: "Classic Football" by
  tuxwrench, https://openclipart.org/detail/305104 (downloaded from
  https://openclipart.org/download/305104), licence CC0 1.0 (public domain
  dedication).

What we took from it: the flat, bold read of a classic ball seen face-on
(a pentagon facing the viewer, five pentagons off its corners cut by the
rim, white hexagons between, ink outlines). The game ball is redrawn from
simple polar geometry, with the game's ink rim, drop shadow and a fixed
light, in four kits: classic black and white, Brazil yellow and green,
laced leather, and Brazil blue.
