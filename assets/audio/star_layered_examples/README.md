# Layered star pickup auditions

Ten short compositions built from twelve ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2 source takes: four warm bell bodies, four rising chimes, and four subdued metallic glints. `tool/compose_star_pickups.py` aligns and mixes the layers, adds a quiet low note, softens the top end, and writes both standalone cues and eight-pickup previews.

These are audition files. The game uses its original three rotating star pickup sounds at a quieter playback level. The third star of a trio uses a normal pickup sound, with no separate completion cue.
